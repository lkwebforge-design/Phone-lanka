Phone buying guides

A practical checklist before you pay a seller.

1. Inspect a used phone in person

  • Check the screen for dead pixels, burn-in, touch issues and cracks.
  • Test cameras, speakers, microphones, buttons, vibration, Wi-Fi and mobile data.
  • Check charging port, battery health where available, and overheating.
  • Confirm storage, RAM, model number and included accessories match the listing.
  • Ask for a written receipt and any warranty terms.

2. Check identity and ownership

  • Dial *#06# to display the IMEI and compare it with the device settings and box where available.
  • Check the IMEI through the relevant official Sri Lankan authority or carrier channel before purchase.
  • Ask the seller to remove their Apple ID, Google account and device locks in front of you.
  • Do not buy a device that is activation-locked, reported lost or has unclear ownership.

3. Buy safely

  • Meet in a public, well-lit place or at the seller's established shop.
  • Inspect and test the exact device before making payment.
  • Avoid advance transfers to unknown sellers and never share OTPs or passwords.
  • Keep the seller's contact details, listing screenshots and payment receipt.
  • Report suspicious or misleading listings through PhoneLanka.

4. Understand condition grades

  • New: Ask whether the phone is sealed, locally authorised and covered by a valid warranty.
  • Used: Check wear, repairs, battery health and whether parts have been replaced.
  • Refurbished: Ask who refurbished it, which parts were changed and what warranty applies.
  • Do not rely on the grade alone; verify the actual phone and paperwork.
